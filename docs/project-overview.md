# Project Overview

Welcome to the Neuroaesthetic Research Project.

This project explores how art, aesthetics, and neuroscience may influence healing, resilience, trauma recovery, accessibility, and human wellbeing.

## Why This Matters

Research suggests that aesthetic experiences can influence stress regulation, emotional processing, cognitive function, and overall health.
